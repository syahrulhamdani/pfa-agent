pfa: Product Requirements

pfa (personal finance assistant) is a Telegram-first agent plus a web app that turns uploaded receipts and statements into confirmed, aggregated data, then nudges, summarizes and recommends for one person managing their own money.

The Core Journey

Scope said "chat, upload, see analytics". Now each step is concrete.

Green: TelegramOutlined: web appGray: proactive messages from pfa

Behavior by Area

What happens. Receipts (images) and statements (PDF, CSV, Excel) are accepted. pfa acknowledges instantly, keeps chatting, then replies to that exact upload with the extraction.
Acceptance criteria
  • Sending a receipt gets an immediate "processing" reply.
  • A second upload or casual question is answered while the first processes.
  • The result arrives threaded to the original upload.
Edge cases
  • Blurry receipt: pfa returns what it read and asks you to enter the unreadable fields.
  • Statement: many transactions, each extracted into the same fields.

Empty state
Screens with no data show a short "nothing here yet" line (assumption).
Cold start: six slots a day, including breakfast, lunch and dinner. Later: slots follow the timestamps of past transactions.
Acceptance criteria
  • With no history, nudges arrive in six daily slots.
  • With history, timing reflects when past transactions occurred.
  • Ignoring a nudge produces no follow-up.
Telegram: a short Monday summary with a link to the dashboard. Web app: last week's stats, category stats, and recommendations against the remaining budget.
Acceptance criteria
  • The Telegram summary is short and links to the dashboard.
  • The dashboard shows stats, categories and recommendations.
  • Asking pfa for insights in chat answers from confirmed data.
For the demo video, the summary and nudge must be triggerable on demand (assumption; mechanism decided in the spec).

Look and Feel

Theme taken from Deloitte's AI & Data page: white backgrounds, dark gray text, light gray dividers, one accent color, clean sans-serif type, modular cards, and a professional tone. The accent color in this page is a placeholder; the exact color and font get confirmed in the spec.

Boundary

What we're building
  • Telegram chat with casual Q&A for insights and recommendations
  • Upload and extraction (images, PDF, CSV, Excel) with confirmation
  • Multiple cards under each bank account
  • Web app: Today's transactions, dashboard, budgets, goals
  • Weekly summary, plus quarterly and yearly insights
  • Adaptive nudges, triggerable for the demo
Deferred from the POC
  • Investment portfolios (excluded by you; next after the PoC)
  • WhatsApp; email digest
Non-goals
  • Bank integration
  • Saving unconfirmed extractions

Open Questions

  • Before the spec: where do bank accounts and cards get created (web app, chat, or on first mention)?
  • Before the spec: for a statement with many rows, confirm each one or in a batch?
  • Confirm: ignoring a nudge counts as "no data", not as a recorded zero.
  • Can wait: exact accent color and font; where quarterly and yearly insights live.
