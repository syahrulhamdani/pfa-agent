Personal Finance Agent

A Telegram-first agent that turns uploaded receipts and statements into live analytics, then nudges, summarizes and recommends so one person can run their own money.

The Unique Kernel

Recommendations and insights that adapt to the running transactions: weekly summaries that reflect next week's budget, budget and goal suggestions from real spending, and an adaptive nudge. With no bank integration, the agent learns when you usually transact from past timestamps and asks you to upload, or to confirm that nothing was spent.

Cold start: six slots a day around meals. Tap a slot to see the nudge.

Who It's For

The learner themself: managing their own money across several banks, multiple cards per bank account and investment portfolios, with no bank integration. Today that means tracking and categorizing by hand.

The Core Loop

Each week starts with a summary on the web app and Telegram: last week's stats, category breakdown, and recommendations against the remaining budget. Between uploads, nudges bring you back.

What "Working" Looks Like

A one-minute demo video:

  1. Upload in Telegram. Chat feels human. The agent replies when processed, with a link to the web app.
  2. Web app. Explore aggregated data, then manage budgets and financial goals.
  3. Back to Telegram. Monday summary, a nudge, and recommendations arrive.
Step 3 is triggered on demand for the demo, not by waiting for a real Monday.

The "oh, that's cool" beat: the receipt becomes live analytics, then the agent speaks up on its own with advice grounded in that data.

Why This Matters

A tool for the learner's own finances, and a way to learn how to work with coding agents without heavy prompting.
